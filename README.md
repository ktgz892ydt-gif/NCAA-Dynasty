# Dynasty HQ

An interactive stats hub for our NCAA Online Dynasty — a single web page that turns the league
spreadsheet into something you can actually browse and share.

It tracks three teams — **Alex (UMass)**, **Blake (Army)**, and **Nick (UConn)** — across every
played season, and lets you compare them head-to-head and see how they've trended over time.

## What's inside

- **Standings** — team cards and a full league leaderboard, with a filter to switch between
  season totals and per-game stats.
- **Head-to-Head** — compare all three teams on any stat, plus a radar "team profile" that shows
  each team's overall shape at a glance.
- **Trends** — how any stat has moved season by season.

Pick a period (career or a single season) and flip between light and dark mode. Works on desktop
and phone.

## Updating

The numbers are a snapshot from the dynasty spreadsheet. To refresh with new data, a new
`index.html` is generated from the updated sheet and uploaded here to replace the old one — the
live link stays the same and updates automatically.
