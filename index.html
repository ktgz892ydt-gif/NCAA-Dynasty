Western Michigan · 9–3

Explosive offense and four straight wins fueled Western’s strong season. Despite an easier-than-average schedule, its 10-point average scoring advantage gave the Broncos the strongest opponent-adjusted performance of the three schools.

Eastern Michigan · 4–8

Eastern stayed competitive in several losses but struggled to score and protect the ball. Its modest average scoring deficit came against an easier-than-average schedule, while a season-ending shutout offered a bright spot.

Central Michigan · 10–2

Central closed with eight straight wins and swept both Michigan rivals. Strong scoring defense helped secure the best record, though an easier schedule and several close wins tempered how dominant the season looked.
