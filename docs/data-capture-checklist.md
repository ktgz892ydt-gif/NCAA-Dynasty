# End-of-season capture checklist

- [ ] Record season year, game-length settings and capture date/stage.
- [ ] Capture every national scoreboard game, including postseason if that is the intended scope.
- [ ] Identify weeks, home/away/neutral sites and overtime consistently.
- [ ] Capture complete offense, defense, situational and special-teams screens.
- [ ] Use each dynasty team's scope for its full individual-stat lists, not only NATIONAL.
- [ ] Scroll vertically to the end and horizontally through every relevant column.
- [ ] Record the sort measure and any player-list cap. A 400-player list does not cover every defender.
- [ ] Capture complete team defense lists to support team TFL totals.
- [ ] Preserve school/player identification evidence; initials alone are ambiguous.
- [ ] Capture championship, bowl and final ranking outcomes.
- [ ] State separately when statistical and postseason-result coverage end.
- [ ] Back up original photos/videos under the correct season before transcribing.
- [ ] Record contradictions, missing frames and retained workbook entries in source notes.
- [ ] Reconcile totals and displayed identities before publication.

Keep zero distinct from missing. Do not infer complete team totals by summing an incomplete national leaderboard. Never merge player careers by abbreviated name alone.
